## 2021 Pacific Northwest heat dome (Oregon)

| Figure | Exact wording or value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Multnomah County deaths, 2021 | 72 heat deaths in 2021; 69 of them from the June 25 to 28 heat dome | Multnomah County, June 27 2022: https://www.multco.us/help-when-its-hot/news/2021-heat-killed-72-people-multnomah-county-most-were-older-lived-alone-had |
| Who died | "78% were 60 or older; 71% lived alone." Only 10 death investigations mentioned a cooling unit in the home; 7 of those were malfunctioning or unplugged. | same |
| Preliminary review (July 2021) | 54 confirmed hyperthermia deaths reviewed at the time; "Of all those who died in their homes, 78 percent lived alone."; none had central air, 8 had portable units and at least 7 of those were unplugged or not working | Multnomah County preliminary review: https://www.multco.us/multnomah-county/news/preliminary-review-heat-wave-deaths-shows-most-were-older-lived-alone-had-no |
| Oregon statewide (interim) | "As of July 26, 2021, a confirmed 83 Oregonians ranging in age from 37 to 97 had tragically lost their lives to hyperthermia, or elevated body temperature; most lived alone in homes with no working air conditioning or fans." | State of Oregon Initial After-Action Review of the June 2021 Excessive Heat Event, Oregon OEM, July 27 2021: https://www.oregon.gov/oem/Documents/2021_June_Excessive_Heat_Event_AAR.pdf |
| Neighbors checking on neighbors | "We know that neighbors checking on neighbors saved lives during the heat emergency. Oregonians should be encouraged to continue to check on their neighbors and reach out to friends, co-workers, and family who may have medical conditions, transportation needs, insufficient housing, inadequate heating or cooling, or other factors that make them more susceptible to extreme weather and a changing climate." | same AAR, recommendation 8 "Neighbors Helping Neighbors" |

Note: later state counts for Oregon were higher than 83. We only cite the figure in the primary document we read.

## Facts about Porchlight itself (from the code in this repo)

| Fact | Where |
|---|---|
| Sentinel polls every 15 minutes; activation thresholds: heat index at or above 105 F, US AQI at or above 151, air temperature at or below 15 F | `backend/app/config.py` (`SENTINEL_INTERVAL_MINUTES`, `HEAT_INDEX_ACTIVATE_F`, `AQI_ACTIVATE`, `COLD_ACTIVATE_F`) |
| NWS event names are mapped deterministically to hazard types heat, air_quality, cold, winter, flood, storm, other; marine, fog and similar advisories are ignored before any model runs | `backend/app/feeds/nws.py` (`_EVENT_MAP`, `_IGNORE_EVENTS`) |
| Hazard types the data model supports | heat, cold, air_quality, storm, flood, winter, outage, other (`backend/app/models.py`, `HazardType`) |
| Demo roster | 12 fictional neighbors, 4 volunteers, real Maryvale (Phoenix) cooling centers and the Maricopa County 2-1-1 heat relief line (`backend/app/seed.py`) |

## Things we looked for and did not use

- A final statewide Oregon death toll for the 2021 heat dome. News reports give higher figures than the 83 in the OEM after-action review; we did not read a primary state document with a final count, so we cite only the interim 83.
- A "lived alone" percentage for Maricopa County deaths. The county report does not publish one; most Maricopa deaths are outdoors, so we do not describe Maricopa's victims as "alone at home".
- Enrollment counts for Be a Buddy in Hunts Point. Not published on the pages we read.
