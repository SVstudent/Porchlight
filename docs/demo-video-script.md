## Editing rules

- Never show a loading spinner longer than 2 seconds. Speed up model thinking 4x with a visible badge.
- Zoom sequences are 3 seconds or less: in, hold, out.
- Label every replay as an archived alert and every manual report as a manual report. Nothing on screen may look live when it is not.
- Cold open text should be white on black, one line at a time, no music until beat 3.
- If a beat runs long, cut beat 11 first, then shorten beat 9 to two hazards. Never cut beat 7.
